The Bebas Neue and Michroma font files are unmodified copies from the pinned
Google Fonts revision recorded in fonts/sources.json. Their SIL Open Font
License notices are retained in fonts/BebasNeue-OFL.txt and fonts/Michroma-OFL.txt.
Generated font artwork uses these fonts; the renderer's MIT license does not
replace their font license.

The Omega symbol and wordmark are third-party brand artwork. The source URL is
recorded in remaster-sources.json. The original vector geometry is preserved;
white paint and proportional placement are adapted for the classic HUD. The
mark is not licensed as project-authored MIT artwork. Brand ownership remains
with its owner, and this personal compatibility port implies no endorsement.

The remaster is unfinished. Local renderer tests are distinct from original
race-mode and physical iPad acceptance. Panels, record plaque, other interface
fonts and final single-player/split-screen spacing remain under development.

Small gold HUD labels and record numerals use the unmodified Oxanium variable
font at weight 500. Its upstream SIL Open Font License is included as
Oxanium-OFL.txt; artwork generation uses the digest-pinned project font.

The countdown sphere is newly authored vector artwork. Its smooth coverage and
shading preserve the classic circular layout; the game retains control of
countdown colors, positions and timing. No original sprite pixels are included.

The eight English main-menu labels use newly authored complete-word vectors:
Oxanium at weight 500 with uniform horizontal condensation, and the naturally
condensed Bebas Neue for RACE. Font licensing remains SIL OFL. Their catalog
contains scalar compatibility positions and interactive bounds, not original
font bitmap data. The optional separate atlas preserves original menu colors
and falls back to the original font for unsupported sources and placements.
